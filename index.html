VALC Drive Train Solutions for Komatsu
930E-4 & 930E-5 Trucks

Alternate OEM drive train components under the VALC brand, built to fit Komatsu 930E-4 and 930E-5 haul trucks.

JAE is the exclusive VALC dealer in Southern Africa, supplying parts already proven on mine sites around the world.

Our Offering
New Alternate OEM Components

New VALC units made to VALC material and process standards, as a direct alternative to the original OEM part.

Rebuild Components

Worn drive train units are stripped, inspected and rebuilt to OEM standard with VALC parts, extending service life at a lower total cost.

Exchange Units

Rebuilt VALC exchange units are held ready. Send in the worn unit and take a rebuilt one to cut downtime.

BLASTHOLE DRILL COMPONENTS

SURFACE DRILL COMPONENT SOLUTIONS

Engineered alternative components for Epiroc DM30 and Pit Viper drills, built for longer life and lower lifecycle cost.

Each part is redesigned in-house from failure analysis. Typical changes are better metallurgy, stronger high-stress areas and tougher wear faces.

Fit, form and function match the original part, so components swap on site with no modification.

The engineering team works with each site on its duty cycle and repeat failures to lift drill availability.

Supply is available new, on exchange, or as a rebuild or repair.
