UNDERGROUND MINING PRODUCTS

Ferrit is a privately owned Czech manufacturer, founded in 1993, working on mines around the world. It began as a trading business and now designs and builds its own equipment with an in-house R&D team.

Suspended monorail transport systems are its main product line, and Ferrit is one of the leading makers of them worldwide.

Zero Harm Values and S.H.E.Q Standards:

Suspended, ground, rail and wheeled vehicles move people, material and muck safely in ore mines, gassy coal mines and tunnel construction.


Development and production

Specialist know-how and modern manufacturing go into every product.

Transport system optimization

Each transport system is designed around the mine's conditions and the customer's needs, for fast, reliable haulage at lower running cost, with better safety and cleaner underground air.
